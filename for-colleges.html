The Challenge

Your Students Want to Transfer.
The System Is Letting Them Down.

Despite strong intentions, the path from community college to a bachelor's degree is filled with barriers that most institutions lack the bandwidth to address — leaving millions of students without the degree they came for.

~33%
Transfer completion rate

Despite 80% of students intending to transfer, fewer than 1 in 3 ever makes it to a 4-year university.

441:1
Advisor overload

Your counselors are stretched too thin to provide the specialized, step-by-step transfer guidance each student needs.

$000s
Unclaimed financial aid

Transfer students routinely miss scholarship and grant opportunities due to lack of expert navigation support.

What's Really Blocking Transfers?

  • Advisor bandwidth — 441:1 ratio leaves no room for specialized transfer guidance
  • Credit opacity — students don't know what will transfer or count
  • Invisible options — online and hybrid degrees that fit working adults' lives aren't easy to find
  • Financial confusion — transfer-specific scholarships and aid go unclaimed
  • No post-acceptance support — students are on their own after the application

This isn't a student motivation problem. It's a support infrastructure problem — and it's exactly what STE was built to solve.

Spotlight: Hybrid + Residency

The Format That
Actually Drives Completion
